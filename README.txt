Road Designer
=============

I. Description of the project
-----

This project is meant to enable users to access a satellite view
of the world, and to draw road layouts on it.

Specifically targeted at urbanists, bicycle infrastructure advocates,
or people who wish to see a change in their city, we want to enable
people to offer a new view of what their street and city could be.

To do so, they can use drawing tools to apply new infrastructure over
the existing landscape, using several tools.
